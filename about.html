A Tradition of Excellence

Established in 1995, AF Confectionery began as a small artisanal kitchen with a singular mission: to create the finest, most luxurious khoya toffees using authentic recipes and premium ingredients.

Today, we stand as a symbol of quality, craftsmanship, and innovation. Every confection is handcrafted by master artisans, blending traditional techniques with modern creativity.

Our commitment to purity means we use only the finest nuts, pure desi ghee, and natural flavors — never any artificial preservatives.
